Thank you - what this study was really about

The AI answers, and especially the "5 of 5 AI models agree" displays, were curated illustrations created by the researchers - not live outputs from five independent models. Each task was also deliberately underspecified: it left out one piece of information (for example, whether "Q1" follows a calendar or fiscal year) needed to be sure the answer is right. So in every task, the appropriate response was to notice that key information was missing.

We study whether a unanimous "models agree" display makes people more likely to accept an answer and feel more confident, and whether telling people the answers are not independent helps. Different people saw the same tasks with different displays, so we can compare fairly.

Your responses are stored under your anonymous ID only. If you would prefer we not use your data, you may withdraw it with no penalty (see the study listing / contact the researcher with your anonymous ID). You will still be paid.
