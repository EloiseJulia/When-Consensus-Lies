# IRB / ethics protocol summary (DRAFT) — human-reliance study

Adapt to the Chang'an University IRB/ethics form. Placeholders `‹…›` need the real values.
Study is minimal-risk with **authorized (mild) deception + full debrief**; likely **exempt/expedited**.

- **Title:** Do people over-rely on unanimous multi-model AI "consensus," and does disclosing
  evidential dependence reduce it?
- **PI / contact:** ‹PI name›, ‹dept›, Chang'an University, ‹email›. **Do not launch before IRB
  determination.**
- **Purpose:** Test whether a "5 of 5 AI models agree" display raises acceptance/confidence in an
  answer to an underspecified task relative to a single-model answer (H-U1), and whether disclosing that
  the models are not independent reduces this (H-U2). Companion to the system study *When Consensus Lies*.
- **Design/procedures:** Online, ~10 min, single session (Prolific). Consent → instructions →
  comprehension check → 12 short trials (each: a workplace scenario + task + an AI answer shown as
  single-model / "5/5 agree" / dependence-disclosed; participant chooses use-as-is vs flag-missing-info,
  optionally picks a clarifying question, and rates confidence 0–100) → 1 attention check → brief
  demographics → **debrief**.
- **Participants:** ‹~65 recruited, ~55 analyzable›; adults ≥18; fluent English; ≥95% platform approval.
  No vulnerable populations targeted.
- **Recruitment/compensation:** Prolific; ≈ ‹$2.00› for ~10 min (≈ $12/hr), platform-standard.
- **Risks:** Minimal — no more than everyday computer use; brief, non-sensitive judgments; no distressing
  content. **Deception (mild):** the AI "answers" and the "5/5 agreement" are **curated illustrations**,
  not live model outputs; participants may assume they are real. **Justification:** the research question
  (response to *apparent* unanimous consensus) cannot be studied without presenting controlled consensus
  displays; risk is minimal and there is no reasonable non-deceptive alternative. **Mitigation:** the
  consent form states some study details are withheld until the end; a **full debrief** discloses the
  curation and purpose and offers data withdrawal.
- **Benefits:** No direct benefit; contributes to understanding trust in multi-model AI interfaces.
- **Privacy/data:** No direct identifiers collected; only an anonymous platform ID (used for payment and
  duplicate detection, then separable). Data stored de-identified on ‹secure store›; retained ‹N years›;
  aggregate/anonymized results may be shared publicly (e.g., OSF) with the paper.
- **Voluntariness:** Fully voluntary; withdraw anytime without penalty; may request data deletion at
  debrief via the anonymous ID.
- **Pre-registration:** Hypotheses, design, primary DVs, exclusions, and analysis frozen on
  OSF/AsPredicted **before** data collection (`PREREGISTRATION.md`, `analysis/`).
