Please read this before deciding to take part.

**What is this study?** We are studying how people read and evaluate answers from AI assistants when a
task could be interpreted in more than one way. You will complete a short set of everyday work-style
judgments.

**What will I do?** After a brief instruction screen and a comprehension check, you will see **12 short
scenarios**. Each shows a task and an AI-assistant answer; you will decide whether the answer can be used
as-is or whether important information is missing, sometimes pick a follow-up question, and rate your
confidence on a slider. It takes about **10 minutes**.

**Withheld information.** So the study works, **some details about its exact purpose are not described
here; they will be fully explained at the end.** Nothing you do carries any risk beyond ordinary computer
use.

**Risks & benefits.** Risks are minimal — the tasks are brief and non-sensitive. There is no direct
benefit to you; your responses help research on how people use AI tools.

**Compensation.** You will receive ‹$2.00› via ‹Prolific› on completion, per the platform's terms.

**Confidentiality.** We do **not** collect your name or contact details. We record only your anonymous
‹Prolific› ID (for payment and to prevent duplicate participation) and your task responses. Data are
stored de-identified and reported only in aggregate. Anonymized data may be shared publicly with the
research (e.g., on OSF).

**Voluntary participation.** Taking part is entirely voluntary. You may stop at any time by closing the
window without penalty. At the end you will be told how to withdraw your data if you wish.
