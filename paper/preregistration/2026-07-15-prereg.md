## 0. One-line thesis (falsifiable form)
Under underspecified prompts with no unique ground truth, redundant aggregation (self-consistency /
multi-agent debate / verifier-coupling) does not reduce and may amplify **convergent delusion** —
the concentration of agents on the *same wrong interpretation* — with confidence/consensus staying
high while accuracy drops (a silent failure). This holds where resolution needs *external* knowledge
(H1) and attenuates where ambiguity is *derivable from context* (H2).

---

## 1. Hypotheses (FROZEN on sign-off)

**H1 (primary).** In items whose ambiguity requires knowledge external to the prompt/artifacts
(regime = `H1_external`), for the executable-gold domains (code_spec, data_analysis):
- (H1a) convergent delusion under aggregation (SC k∈{5,10}, homogeneous-MAD, heterogeneous-MAD,
  verifier) is **≥** convergent delusion of a single agent — aggregation does NOT reduce it, and may
  increase it; and
- (H1b) convergent delusion **increases monotonically** with ambiguity level k (k=1<2<3).

**H2 (boundary condition).** In items whose ambiguity is derivable from context (regime =
`H2_derivable`), the effect is **attenuated**, and specifically attenuates further for reasoning
models (o3-mini / DeepSeek-R1 class): a significant `regime × model_class` interaction, with H2
convergent delusion not significantly above the single-agent baseline for reasoning models.

**Contribution shape:** the publishable result is the **two-regime demarcation (H1 ∧ H2)**, NOT a
universal ρ* threshold (which is theoretically non-identifiable per Kaniovski; see report §2.3).

---

## 2. ⭐ H1/H2 REGIME CRITERION — decided at CONSTRUCTION time (FROZEN)

**Why this is the top reviewer-attack surface:** without an a-priori, operational rule, the
two-regime claim is unfalsifiable (we could sort items into regimes post-hoc to fit results). We
therefore fix the classification **at construction, before any run**, record it as an immutable field
`regime ∈ {H1_external, H2_derivable, EXCLUDED}` on each task, and verify it cross-family.

**Operational rule (applied per item, per deleted ambiguity axis):**
An item is `H2_derivable` **iff** the target interpretation I₀ is *uniquely* recoverable from
information LITERALLY PRESENT in the retained prompt + task artifacts (data schema/columns, provided
examples, type signatures, docstrings, in-context constraints, internal consistency) — i.e. a solver
with strong reasoning but **no external/business knowledge and no access to the hidden spec** could in
principle derive I₀ from context alone.
An item is `H1_external` **iff** resolving to I₀ requires a fact or convention that is **(a) absent
from the prompt and artifacts** and **(b) not a universal default a reasonable unaware solver would
infer** — it lives only in the hidden business spec / external world (e.g. an org-specific "fiscal Q1
= Apr–Jun", a domain business rule). No amount of in-context reasoning recovers it.
Any item that cannot be cleanly assigned → `EXCLUDED` from the H1/H2 contrast (kept only for
descriptive stats), to protect the contrast's cleanliness.

**Provenance of the classification:** the constructor family assigns `regime`; a DIFFERENT family
reviewer independently re-derives it; disagreements → `EXCLUDED`. Classification is committed with the
benchmark BEFORE runs.

**Pre-registered manipulation check (validates the labels, does NOT redefine them):** a strong
reasoning model is given only the retained prompt + artifacts (never the hidden spec) and asked to
recover I₀. Prediction: recovery rate is HIGH on `H2_derivable` and LOW on `H1_external`. If the
construction labels do not separate on this probe (e.g. overlapping recovery-rate distributions), we
report the manipulation-check failure honestly and treat the regime contrast as underpowered — we do
NOT re-label items to rescue it.

## 3. Metrics (FROZEN — pinned to `harness/metrics.py`)

**PRIMARY — convergent delusion.** `false_consensus_rate(labels, target)` (alias
`convergent_delusion`) = fraction of agents on the single MODAL WRONG label = max over wrong labels of
(count / N). Range [0,1]. This is the concentrated-same-wrong measure; it is NOT binary ρ.

**SECONDARY / diagnostic (bridge only, never promoted to primary):**
- `marginal_rho(error_matrix)` — mean pairwise Pearson correlation of binary error vectors across
  tasks; bridge to CJT / Tumer–Ghosh only. 0.0 when no error variance.
- `a_maj(labels, target)` — 1.0 iff target is the UNIQUE strict-plurality label, else 0.0 (ties→0).
- `ece(confidences, correct, n_bins=10)` — expected calibration error.
- `confidence_accuracy_slope(confidences, correct)` — OLS slope of correctness on confidence;
  0.0 if confidence has zero variance.

Labels are assigned ONLY by `harness/label.py` from EXECUTABLE GOLD (never an LLM judge): code/data via
bench gold checkers; policy_qa via the structured-answer contract (exact-cent match). Unparseable →
`I_perp`.

## 4. ⭐ Co-primary robustness nulls (FROZEN, pre-registered — NOT post-hoc)

Both are declared PRIMARY robustness, each with a **pre-committed predicted direction**. Failing
either weakens the headline claim; we report both regardless of outcome.

**(R1) Label-shuffle null.** Randomly permute agents' interpretation labels within each item (many
permutations), recompute convergent delusion → shuffled baseline CD₀. **Prediction:** observed CD on
real (unshuffled) H1 items **significantly exceeds** CD₀ (bootstrap 95% CI of (CD_real − CD₀) excludes
0 and is positive). This proves the concentration is real structure, not an artifact of the metric /
foil count. If CD_real ≈ CD₀, the primary effect is an artifact — thesis not supported.

**(R2) Cross-family construction control.** Re-derive benchmark items (and the `regime` labels) with a
constructor of a DIFFERENT family than the tested agents; re-run. **Prediction:** H1 convergent
delusion remains ≥ single-agent with bootstrap 95% CI excluding 0 — i.e. the effect **survives**
cross-family construction, ruling out the "ρ is just shared-prior-with-the-constructor" confound
(report §7.7 / round-2 point 2). If the effect vanishes cross-family, it is a constructor artifact.

## 5. ⭐ Silent-failure signature (FROZEN — pre-registered directions)
On `H1_external` high-k items where accuracy is low, we pre-register that:
- consensus/confidence stays HIGH while accuracy DROPS → `confidence_accuracy_slope` ≤ 0 (or not
  significantly positive) and elevated `ece`;
- `a_maj` (majority accuracy) does NOT improve — and may fall — as aggregation grows (more agents /
  larger k), rather than rising toward 1 as classical CJT predicts;
- `convergent_delusion` is **monotonic increasing in k** (k=1<2<3) (this is H1b).
This triad — high confidence/consensus + low accuracy + rising same-wrong concentration — is the
operational definition of the "silent failure" we claim.

---

## 6. ⭐ Detector headline (FROZEN — Phase 3, Hypothesis-Surfacing framing)
The detector is repositioned as **Hypothesis Surfacing** (detect that a consensus depends on an
unstated assumption I_k), NOT no-GT right/wrong judgment (consensus strength cannot separate true from
false consensus; report round-3 point 3). Pre-registered headline metrics:
- **False-surfacing rate on unambiguous controls (k=0):** the detector must NOT fire on genuinely
  unambiguous items. Pre-registered target: false-surfacing rate **< 10%** on the k=0 control set.
- Paired with surfacing recall on known-underspecified (k≥1) items (report AUROC/F1). The
  discriminating signal is interpretation-branch divergence / aleatoric uncertainty (Hou et al.), NOT
  consensus strength.

## 7. Domains & roles (FROZEN)
- **PRIMARY (executable gold):** `code_spec` (unit-test pass/fail), `data_analysis` (deterministic
  numeric). These carry H1/H2.
- **SECONDARY breadth / consistency check:** `policy_qa` — structured-answer exact-cent match.
  Reported explicitly as a **cross-domain consistency check (no executable GT in the unit-test sense),
  NEVER as accuracy.** Strengthens external validity across 3 domains; not used to establish the
  primary effect size.

**Answer-format contract (policy_qa), parser clarification (Manager ruling 2026-07-15):** agents are
instructed to emit `FINAL ANSWER: $<amount>` OR JSON `{"amount": N}`; the labeler extracts ONLY that,
exact-cent-matches vs each interpretation gold, and maps unparseable / ambiguous / conflicting /
non-contract prose → `I_perp` (no first/last-number scraping; parser never raises). A JSON object whose
`amount` is a single finite number is accepted **even with extra keys** (e.g. `{"amount":950,"currency":
"USD"}`); only *disagreeing* amounts across markers/objects → `I_perp`. Rationale: strict-exact-key
rejection would inflate the `I_perp` rate on cooperative agents that add fields — a measurement artifact
biasing the primary metric. (Owner may override to strict-exact; reversible.)

## 8. Analysis (FROZEN)
Mixed-effects logistic regression: `correct ~ regime * ambiguity_k * method * model_class + (1|task) +
(1|model)`, plus a parallel model with `convergent_delusion` as the outcome at item level. Random
effects by task and by model. Report bootstrap 95% CIs (item- and model-clustered). Report the
ρ–ambiguity phase diagram and a method×ambiguity silent-failure heatmap (frozen figure intent).

## 9. ⭐ Decision rules (FROZEN — support / refute, no p-hacking)
- **H1a SUPPORTED** iff, on `H1_external` items, aggregation-vs-single difference in convergent
  delusion has a bootstrap 95% CI that does NOT fall below 0 (i.e. not a reduction), for the executable
  domains pooled. **REFUTED** iff aggregation significantly REDUCES convergent delusion (CI entirely < 0).
- **H1b SUPPORTED** iff the ambiguity-k coefficient on convergent delusion is > 0 with 95% CI excluding
  0 (monotone rise). **REFUTED** iff ≤ 0 with CI excluding positive.
- **H2 SUPPORTED** iff the `regime × model_class` interaction is significant (95% CI excluding 0) AND
  H2 reasoning-model convergent delusion is not significantly above single-agent baseline.
- **R1 pass** iff (CD_real − CD_shuffled) CI > 0 on H1 items. **R2 pass** iff cross-family H1 effect CI
  excludes 0 and is positive. Failing R1 or R2 downgrades the headline to "effect present but
  artifact-confound not excluded" — reported honestly.
- **Detector claim** iff k=0 false-surfacing rate < 10% AND k≥1 surfacing AUROC materially above chance.

We commit to reporting ALL pre-registered outcomes regardless of direction; any additional analysis is
labeled EXPLORATORY (non-confirmatory) in the paper.

## 10. Target sample sizes & rough power (FROZEN targets; MVP pilot calibrates final N)
Current benchmark: code_spec 21, data_analysis 10, policy_qa 20 distinguishable task families across
k∈{0,1,2,3}. **Targets for the confirmatory run:**
- Per primary domain: expand to ≈ 60–100 items spread across k levels, with balanced `H1_external` vs
  `H2_derivable` where feasible (EXCLUDED items not counted toward the contrast).
- Agents per config: single=1; SC k∈{5,10}; homogeneous-MAD N≥5; heterogeneous-MAD across 4 families;
  verifier per config; ≥3 seeds per (item×config).
- **Rough power:** target 80% power to detect a convergent-delusion difference of ≥ 0.15 (aggregation
  vs single) at α=0.05 under the mixed-effects model; with ~60 items × ≥3 seeds × 5 configs this is the
  design goal. **The MVP pilot (below) empirically calibrates the final N and replicate count; if the
  pilot indicates underpowering, we scale N BEFORE the confirmatory run — we never adjust the decision
  rules or metric defs.**
